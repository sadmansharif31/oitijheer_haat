About ঐতিহ্যের হাট

ঐতিহ্যের হাট is a heritage fair that brings the best of all 64 districts of Bangladesh to one campus. DIU students set up stalls for their home districts and sell the products each district is famous for: traditional crafts, local food and artisanal goods.

For the first time, all 64 districts will be represented, so visitors can enjoy the whole country's heritage in a single day, and the students gain real business experience.

🏺 Traditional crafts & artisanal goods
🍯 Famous regional foods
🏪 64 student-run district stalls
🎭 Cultural performances
🏆 District competition & awards

Why It Matters

Preserves heritage

Keeps each district's crafts, food and traditions alive.

Builds entrepreneurs

Students learn product sourcing, marketing, selling and customer handling in practice.

Inspires pride

Celebrates Bangladesh's cultural diversity and encourages patriotism.

Connects people

Brings together students, alumni, business leaders and the public.
